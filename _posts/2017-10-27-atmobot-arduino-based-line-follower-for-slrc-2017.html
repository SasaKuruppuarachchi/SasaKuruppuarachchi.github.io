This Arduino based Line follower robot is developed to compete at the Sri Lankan Robotics Challenge 2017 competition held at University of Moratuwa.

There are two robots, autonomous unit and the Controlled unit. Both units cave to work in unison to complete the task.

The robot took the 4th place in the competition.
