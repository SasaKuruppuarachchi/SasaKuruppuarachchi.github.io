Opening a new chapter in Sri Lankan engineering history, president of Institute of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE), Mrs. Carolyn Griffiths visited University of Moratuwa on 17th of November as a part of her tour in Sri Lanka. Team SHARK Racing was most honoured to showcase the award winning 2016 Formula Student car at this special occasion. Special Thanks for all the Honorable guests who attended the event and to DIMO Lanka PLC for all the support. Team SHARK Racing is the leading Sri Lankan Formula Student team, racing for the glory of Sri Lanka.
