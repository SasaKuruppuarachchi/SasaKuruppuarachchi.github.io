Formula Student (FS) is Europe’s most established educational motor-sport competition, run by the Institution of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE). Backed by industry and high profile engineers such as our Patron Ross Brawn OBE, the competition aims to inspire and develop enterprising and innovative young engineers. In this competition, teams are required to design and manufacture an open-wheeled and open-cockpit (a formula style body) car.

Undergraduates from all over the world take part in this competition. Last year, more than 4,000 students from over 32 countries participated in this competition.

DI -MORA P2 is the second Formula student car developed by a Sri Lankan team. I was the Cheif Electrical and Systems Engineer of the 2017-18 team. I designed

and manufactured The Electronic Throttle, the custom Solenoid for Pedal Shift, The Data Acquisition System and the Custom Dash board. Further I designed the Wire harness of the car and did the ECU setup and tuning.
