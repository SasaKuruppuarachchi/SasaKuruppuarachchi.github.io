This is an animated mechanical real size human figure designed based on a gear mechanism. This was a Exhibit at the 2015 inauguration ceremony of University of Moratuwa.

The Gear mechanism is designed by creating curved paths to mimic human movement using sampling in the parameter space.

The project can be seen at the entrance of the Department of mechanical Engineering, University of Moratuwa.
