This prosthetic arm is designed for a amputee who lost the arm above elbow joint (Ulna).

The control signal is obtained from EMG transducers placed close to Bicep and Tricep Branchii. Processing algorithm is used for signal recognition. Three different joints of the arm is controlled by serially selecting for each movement.

This project was an exhibit at TECHNO 2016 exhibition held from 7th to 8th October 2016.
