MSCA Doctoral Fellow · Tampere University

Building agile aerial robots that know where they are.

I'm a doctoral researcher and Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellow in the Computer Vision Group at Tampere University, Finland, within the EU RAICAM doctoral network. I design and prototype agile aerial robots and aerial manipulators, build their digital twins, and develop robust, learning-based state estimation that runs on the edge.
